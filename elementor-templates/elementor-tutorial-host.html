Cashout Walkthrough

How to Withdraw Your Earnings

Step-by-step procedure to exchange your Beans and withdraw funds directly to your preferred payment account.

1

Access Beans Menu

Log in to your host or agent account and open your profile

Your account needs to be verified and is an active host before you can receive your balance.

In the profile you will see your ‘Beans’

2

Select Payout Method

Click the ‘Beans’ menu

Click the ‘Recharge’ button

Click ‘Withdraw’

Choose your preferred methods. We offer bank transfer, PayPal, Epay, Binance, USDT, G-cash, and e-wallet (availability depends on your country)

3

Submit Payout Details

Enter your preferred payout information

If using bank, fill in your name, account number, and bank name

If using PayPal, fill in your PayPal email address

Enter the correct information, otherwise it will fail

Determine the amount you want to exchange to USD

Minimum payout amount is 2,000,000 beans or 200 USD. This amount may change.

After everything is correct, click ‘Submit’

Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions regarding host guidelines, face verification, and withdrawals.

Hosts may only have one account each, and content must never be pornographic or sexual in nature. Promoting third-party platforms is not allowed, and minors are prohibited from livestreaming.

Only hosts with a qualified cover photo will appear on the live page. The photo should look presentable and attractive, set against a clean, bright background. Use a high-quality upper body shot rather than just your face or head.

Before going live, set up a clean and tidy background, show your upper body, and apply makeup, avoid low-quality setups like lying down or filming empty spaces. During the stream, check your video quality periodically and interact with viewers by greeting, talking, thanking them, and encouraging follows or gifts. After the stream ends, reach out to gifters to maintain the relationship and post highlight moments to the community page for more traffic.

From the Niki Live homepage, click Start Live, then Verify Now, and follow the on-screen instructions to complete the scan. Once submitted, verification is reviewed and can take up to 3 business days to complete.

First, make sure your account is verified and active as a host, then open your profile to view your Beans balance. Go to Beans, click Recharge, then Withdraw, and choose a payout method such as bank transfer, PayPal, Epay, Binance, USDT, G-cash, or e-wallet (availability depends on your country). Enter your payout details correctly, set the amount you want to exchange to USD, and click Submit, the minimum payout is 2,000,000 beans (about 200 USD), though this amount may change, and incorrect payout information will cause the withdrawal to fail.
