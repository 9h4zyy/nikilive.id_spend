Host Onboarding

Add New Host as Niki Live Agent

Follow these sequential steps to invite, verify, and register new broadcasting talents directly under your agency roster.

1

Go to your profile, then click the ‘Agency’ menu

2

Click the ‘Add Host’ button

3

Enter the ID of the host you are inviting

4

After the correct host shows up, click ‘Add’

5

The host will receive an invitation message on their own app

6

The host need to accept the invitation and complete the verification process (face and ID verification, if they haven’t)

7

Once they agree, the host will be officially registered with your agency and can start broadcasting.

Sub-Agency Onboarding

How to Add New Sub-agent

Scale your team by inviting sub-agents who run their own host rosters under your primary agency banner.

1

Go to your profile, then click the ‘Agency’ menu

2

Click the ‘Invite Sub-Agent’ button

3

Copy the sub-agent invitation link, give the link to the sub-agent who wants to join.

4

After that, give directions to the sub-agents you want to invite.

a

Go to profile section, “ Copy ID”

b

Enter the ID and click Send

c

The code will be sent via a message on the Niki Live account

d

Copy the code and then click submit
