Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions regarding host registration, earnings, and withdrawals.

Joining Niki Live as a host is easy. Simply download the Niki Live app, register using your mobile number, email, or social media account, complete the required information, and submit your application to start your host journey.

Your earnings depend on your live streaming activities and audience engagement. Hosts can earn through video calls, voice calls, gift sharing, and in-room game revenue. The conversion rate is 10,000 Beans = $1 USD.

Niki Live allows hosts to request withdrawals at any time. Payments are processed weekly, with a minimum withdrawal amount of $10 USD. Available withdrawal methods include Epay, Payoneer, PayPal, and bank transfer, depending on availability and applicable service fees.

Niki Live offers competitive earning opportunities with high gift share rates, additional income from live room games, flexible withdrawals, weekly payments, and various host reward events designed to support both new and experienced hosts.

Host rewards are based on your earnings from the last 7 days, including today and the previous 6 days. As your earnings level increases, your daily reward can also increase.
