Frequently Asked Questions

A Niki Live Coin Seller is a user who can purchase coins from Niki Live and sell them to other users or agents. It allows eligible users to earn additional income through coin trading on the platform.

Your profit depends on who you sell coins to. You can earn around 10%–21% profit when selling coins to users and around 7%–14% profit when selling coins to other agents.

You can become a Coin Seller if you meet one of these conditions:

•

You are an agent earning more than $1,000 per week and make a one-time purchase of $1,000 worth of coins from Niki Live.

•

You make a one-time purchase of $1,600 worth of coins from Niki Live.

You can get trading coins in two ways:

1

Recharge coins through the Coin Seller menu on your profile page by selecting “Recharge Now” and completing the payment process.

2

Exchange your earned Beans into trading coins by selecting “Exchange Bean for Trading Coins” and confirming the amount.

To improve your account visibility, make sure your trading account is properly set up with the correct region, currency, and phone number. Maintaining enough trading coins can also unlock benefits, such as customer management features, the official Coin Seller logo, and additional exposure on the official Coin Seller page.
