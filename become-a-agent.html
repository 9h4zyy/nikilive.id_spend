Compliance & Policies

Niki Live Agency Rules

All agents must adhere strictly to these platform rules to ensure fair and legal operation.

🛑 Anti-poaching

Agents are strictly prohibited from recruiting hosts who belong to other Niki agencies.

1st Offense: $10 penalty.

2nd Offense: $100 penalty.

Repeated Violations: Forfeiture of all commissions and termination of the agency.

🚫 Multi Host & Platform Rules

It is strictly forbidden to promote other apps on Niki, and from joining more than one agency simultaneously

Promoting other apps will result in suspension and eventually permanent ban

Using multiple accounts to join one agency will result the host’s newest account be banned

⚖️ Host Payment & Exchange Rate

Unauthorized deductions from a host’s salary is forbidden, and when paying off hosts’ salary, agents must not exceed 5% of Google’s international exchange rate.

If a verified complaint is received from a host, Niki will stop distributing that host’s income share to the agent.

📋 Management & Liability

Agents must maintain good relationships with hosts. actively manage their live schedules, and make sure they adhere to Niki Live’s rules.

If an agency’s violation causes serious financial losses to Niki, the responsible party will face legal punishment.

Scale Your Team

Niki Live Sub-agent

A sub-agent is a partner operating under your primary agency account. Sub-agent operate as an independent agency with the exact same rights, rules, and privileges as a main agency.

⚡ Benefits

Their revenue is added to your overall weekly income calculation

Allows you to reach higher commission levels much faster.

📌 Notes

Sub-agent commission rate is determined entirely by their own total income.

The commission earned by the main agency from a sub-agency is a bonus provided directly by Niki Live. It is never deducted from the sub-agent’s earnings.
